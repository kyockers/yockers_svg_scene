Cactus Boat logo

I drew the logo for Cactus Boat: a happy little cactus riding in an orange boat, that resembles a plant pot, in front of the company name. The cactus holds a little flag that's waving in the wind. The cactus itself becomes the "t" in "Cactus". The background colors, cactus body, and cactus arms are rectangles with rounded corners. The eyes are circles. The boat shadow is an ellipse. The boat and flag are polygons. The flagpole and center line are lines (center line is hidden for now, but will be used in the animation.)

Reflection: I LOVED making this! My design background really helped me in this assignment becuase I work with .svg shapes pretty often. Getting the pieces to fit wasn't actually too hard, it just took a lot of tweaking. The eyes/arms/body of the cactus took the longest to get right since I worked on them first and they needed to set the scale for the rest of the elements. I used the color palette from my previous Cactus Boat page, so I only had to come up with an accent color for the boat/flag. I tried yellow, orange-red, and brown, but they didn't work so i went with a nice bright orange. The Google homepage uses vector graphics for its logo, and SVG scenes to animate their Google Doodles.
